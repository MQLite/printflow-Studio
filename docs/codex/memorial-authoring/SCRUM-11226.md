## Behavior

- Pure Domain records express millimetre canvases, image/text layers, pixel crop rectangles and future render identities. Domain has no WPF or file I/O dependency.
- Historical presets are T-shirt 280×390 mm and badge 86×86 mm, outer diameter 86 mm and safe diameter 72 mm, giving a 7 mm ring. These are design presets requiring confirmation, not verified device dimensions.
- Geometry validation checks finite positive dimensions, matching badge dimensions, canvas extent and every critical rectangle corner against the circular safe area. Tangency is allowed; centering a rectangle alone is insufficient.
- Original people and decorations require confirmed preservation permissions, full-source aspect ratio or an explicitly permitted crop, and authorized movement/uniform scaling. Explicit geometry denials also apply to backgrounds independently of content permissions. Cropping cannot silently grant zoom permission. Preserved active content cannot disappear or be split across multiple roles, and relabelling the person as a background cannot remove its safety requirement.
- Background and explicitly permitted unprotected generated edge decoration may extend into the badge ring. Preserved content remains critical regardless of its displayed role.
- Text lines use stable semantic confirmation keys, such as `name`, and must contain the exact field value, including spaces, combining marks and line endings. Missing/unknown/duplicate text is reported. This task does not measure or certify glyph fit; oversized names are never truncated here.
- `SetLayout` uses the existing draft revision CAS. Caller collections are detached before the first async read and again on storage. An initial or changed layout is unconfirmed; an unchanged subsequent command can explicitly confirm it only after geometry/permission/text validation. Unauthorized geometry changes and rewritten text are refused.
- Changes to fields, source bindings, preservation rules, template or final prompt revoke layout confirmation. Unchanged text-content hashes survive geometry/style changes. Switching templates clears the old product layout.
- Draft schema 1 reads as schema 2 with null layout and its original fields, documents, image identities, confirmations and revision preserved. Reading never rewrites committed history. The next ordinary CAS save publishes a new schema 2 version. Future draft schemas and layouts smuggled into schema 1 are refused. The authoring storage envelope and old AI Request schema stay unchanged.

## Independent review and final verification

Requested model `gpt-6-astra` with high reasoning; runtime model identity is not independently verified. No reviewer builds, testhosts or child agents were used. No Critical issues; five Important findings:

1. Exact date labels were not representable as separate verified text layers. Add the collision-safe key `date-label:<semantic text key>`, require the exact label when both date and label are nonempty, and omit the whole optional date/label pair when the date is absent. B2 must consume this contract.
2. `KeepContent=false` skipped explicitly denied geometric operations. Content and geometry permissions are independent, including background layers.
